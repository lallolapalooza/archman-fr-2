Detailed Description

This section describes the individual routines. The routines SLAQZ1, SLAQZ2, SLAQZ3, ZLAQZ1, ZLAQZ2, and ZLAQZ3 are used in the QR algorithm to compute the Schur factorization of a generalized eigenvalue problem.

They are designed to chase a bulge (a small localized structure) in the matrix pencil (A,B) down one position.

The main goal of these routines is to transform the matrix pencil (A,B) into an upper triangular form, which simplifies the computation of eigenvalues and eigenvectors.

The routines differ in the type of bulge they handle (1x1 or 2x2) and the data type they operate on (real or complex).

SLAQZ1

Purpose:

!>
!>      Given a 3-by-3 matrix pencil (A,B), SLAQZ1 sets v to a
!>      scalar multiple of the first column of the product
!>
!>      (*)  K = (A - (beta2*sr2 - i*si)*B)*B^(-1)*(beta1*A - (sr2 + i*si2)*B)*B^(-1).
!>
!>      It is assumed that either
!>
!>              1) sr1 = sr2
!>          or
!>              2) si = 0.
!>
!>      This is useful for starting double implicit shift bulges
!>      in the QZ algorithm.
!>

Parameters

A 
!>          A is REAL array, dimension (LDA,N)
!>              The 3-by-3 matrix A in (*).
!>

LDA

!>          LDA is INTEGER
!>              The leading dimension of A as declared in
!>              the calling procedure.
!>

B

!>          B is REAL array, dimension (LDB,N)
!>              The 3-by-3 matrix B in (*).
!>

LDB

!>          LDB is INTEGER
!>              The leading dimension of B as declared in
!>              the calling procedure.
!>

SR1

!>          SR1 is REAL
!>

SR2

!>          SR2 is REAL
!>

SI

!>          SI is REAL
!>

BETA1

!>          BETA1 is REAL
!>

BETA2

!>          BETA2 is REAL
!>

V

!>          V is REAL array, dimension (N)
!>              A scalar multiple of the first column of the
!>              matrix K in (*).
!>
