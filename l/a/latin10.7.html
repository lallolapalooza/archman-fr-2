NOM

iso_8859-16 - Jeu de caractères ISO/IEC 8859-16 encodé en octal, décimal et hexadécimal

DESCRIPTION

La norme ISO/IEC 8859 comprend plusieurs extensions 8 bits au jeu de caractères ASCII (également connu sous le nom d'ISO/IEC 646-IRV). ISO/IEC 8859-16 encode les caractères latins utilisés dans les langues de l'Europe du Sud-Est.

Alphabet ISO/IEC 8859

L'ensemble complet des alphabets ISO/IEC 8859 comprend :

ISO/IEC 8859-1 Langues d'Europe de l'Ouest (Latin-1)
ISO/IEC 8859-2 Langues d'Europe Centrale et de l'Est (Latin-2)
ISO/IEC 8859-3 Langues d'Europe du Sud-Est et diverses (Latin-3)
ISO/IEC 8859-4 Langues scandinaves/baltes (Latin-4)
ISO/IEC 8859-5 Latin/Cyrillique
ISO/IEC 8859-6 Latin/Arabe
ISO/IEC 8859-7 Latin/Grec
ISO/IEC 8859-8 Latin/Hébreu
ISO/IEC 8859-9 Modification de Latin-1 pour le turc (Latin-5)
ISO/IEC 8859-10 Langues lapones/nordiques/esquimaudes (Latin-6)
ISO/IEC 8859-11 Latin/Thaï
ISO/IEC 8859-13 Langues du pourtour baltique (Latin-7)
ISO/IEC 8859-14 Celte (Latin-8)
ISO/IEC 8859-15 Langues d'Europe de l'Ouest (Latin-9)
ISO/IEC 8859-16 Roumain (Latin-10)

NAME

latin10(7)

ISO/IEC 8859-16 standard character set

DESCRIPTION

The latin10 character set, also known as ISO/IEC 8859-16, is an extension of the ISO/IEC 8859 series of 8-bit character encodings. It is defined by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC).

ISO/IEC 8859-16 includes characters for most of the Latin alphabets used in the languages of Europe, with a particular emphasis on Central and Southeastern European languages. It covers characters found in languages such as Albanian, Croatian, Czech, Estonian, Finnish, Hungarian, Irish, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Norwegian, Polish, Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian, Swedish, and Vietnamese.

The character set is structured as a 7-bit ASCII encoding in the lower half (00-7F) and provides an additional 128 characters in the upper half (80-FF).
