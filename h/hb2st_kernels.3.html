NAME


hb2st_kernels (3)


internal routines used by the SYTRD_HB2ST and HETRD_HB2ST
    subroutines.

Detailed Description


The routines are used for the reduction of a real symmetric
    band matrix to tridiagonal form. They are internal routines used by
    SSYTRD and DSYTRD. More specifically, these routines perform the
    reduction of a sub-block of the matrix A. The routines are used in
    the context of the parallel reduction to condensed forms and they
    implement a fine-grained, memory-aware algorithm suitable for multicore
    architectures.


The reduction of a symmetric band matrix A to symmetric tridiagonal
    form is achieved by applying a sequence of Householder
    transformations.


A = Q T Q^T


where T is a tridiagonal matrix. The routine is designed to
    work on a small block of the matrix A, and is called recursively or
    iteratively to reduce the entire matrix.


The implementation is based on the research papers cited in the
    "Further Details" section, focusing on efficient parallel execution on
    modern hardware, particularly leveraging CPU-GPU hybrid approaches where
    applicable.
